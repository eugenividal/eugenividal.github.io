👋 ✨ Hi there, I’m Eugeni!

I am a mobility researcher with a background in environmental management, transport planning and data analytics (and yes, I studied law too!). My work has spanned industry and more recently academia, with a focus on cycling, active travel, transport equity, road safety and transport planning.

Cycling, data visualisation, and maps are key passions of mine, and they drive my interest in better understanding how we move around cities and how we can make mobility more sustainable and accessible.

My current role is as a Postdoctoral Researcher at GEMOTT (the Research Group in Mobility, Transport, and Territory, from its Catalan acronym) in the Department of Geography at the Autonomous University of Barcelona. I primarily work on the ATRAPA project, which looks into the factors influencing public acceptance of active travel interventions in the built environment.

This site is where I share my projects, presentations, and ideas——feel free to take a look!
