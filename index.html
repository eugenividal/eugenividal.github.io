👋 Hi, I’m Eugeni!

I’m a postdoctoral researcher at GEMOTT (the Research Group in Mobility, Transport, and Territory) at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona, where I work on the ATRAPA project, exploring public acceptance of walking and cycling initiatives. I’m also involved in research on active commuting to school and big data mobility.

Previously, I was a Research Fellow in Active Travel and Data Science at the Institute for Transport Studies in Leeds (2022–2024), working with large-scale mobility datasets to support policy and planning.

I completed a PhD in Data Analytics and Society at the University of Leeds, where I studied how cycling relates to socioeconomic disadvantage. Earlier, I spent ten years as a transport planner in Barcelona, focusing on sustainable mobility and road safety.

My background is in environmental management, transport planning, and data analytics — oh, and I studied law too. I’m passionate about cycling, data visualisation, and maps, which drive my research on how people move through cities and how the built environment shapes their travel choices.

This site is where I share my projects, presentations, and ideas—feel free to explore or get in touch at eugenividal [at] hotmail [dot] com.
